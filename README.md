# live a little 🧡

> *Swipe right to take the dare. Left to pass. Right to start actually living.*

**live a little** is a daily challenge app that nudges you out of your comfort zone — one swipe at a time. It's part dare card deck, part personal diary. Every challenge you accept becomes a memory you keep.

---

## What It Is

Most days blur together. live a little breaks the pattern.

You open the app and find a stack of dare cards waiting — some tiny, some nerve-wracking, some just plain fun. Swipe right to accept, left to pass. When you take a dare, you capture it: photos, a note, how it felt. It lives in your Storybook forever — a growing record of the little life you're building.

It's not about being brave. It's about being present.

---

## Core Features

### Discover
Swipe through daily dare cards across five categories — **Social**, **Adventure**, **Creative**, **Kindness**, and **Taste**. Each card shows the dare, how long it takes, and a nerve level so you know what you're getting into.

### Storybook
Your personal memory wall. Every completed dare becomes a polaroid-style entry with your photo and note. The little life you're building, laid out in front of you.

### History
A clean timeline of every dare you've lived — when you did it, what category, and a quick path back to the memory.

### You
Your profile tracks your streak, dares lived, and what you naturally reach for. It tells you something true about yourself: *you're at your happiest being adventurous.*

---

## The Idea Behind It

Somewhere between childhood and adulthood, most people stop doing things just to see what happens. live a little is a gentle shove back in that direction.

The dares aren't extreme. They're the kind of thing you'd tell a friend about later — calling someone you miss, eating dessert first, getting lost on purpose, photographing something the colour of a sunset. Small moves. Real memories.

## Tech Stack

- **React Native** — iOS and Android from one codebase
- **Supabase** — auth, database, photo storage
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management
- **Posthog** — analytics and user behavior
- **OneSignal** — daily dare reminders

---

## Monetization

Free tier includes access to core dare categories. Premium unlocks expanded dare packs, deeper Storybook features, and exclusive category collections. Subscription-based — because the best things in life are worth a few bucks a month.
